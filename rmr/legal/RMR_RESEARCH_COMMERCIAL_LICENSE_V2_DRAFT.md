# RMR Research / Commercial License V2 — DRAFT

**NOT ACTIVE. DO NOT USE AS THE LICENSE FILE.**

This draft is a negotiation artifact for legal review. It does not replace `rmr/LICENSE_RMR`.

## Intended scope

Only RMR-authored material explicitly identified in a future path/license matrix and first released under this license.

Excluded:

- upstream BLAKE3 code, vectors and documentation;
- third-party libraries and providers;
- material previously distributed under prior grants, to the extent applicable.

## Proposed research permission

Subject to final legal review, qualifying non-commercial research may be permitted without a license fee for study, reproducibility, academic benchmarking, teaching, publication of results and modification for research validation.

Conditions may include preservation of copyright/provenance notices, citation of the RMR source/version, no false endorsement, and disclosure of material modifications.

## Proposed commercial restriction

Commercial exploitation would require a separate written commercial license, including commercial distribution, embedding in paid products/services, resale, OEM use or monetized hosted service where RMR is a material component.

The final definition must be negotiated and reviewed; this draft does not attempt to bind third parties merely by repository access.

## Proposed commercial-contract controls

A signed commercial agreement may define license scope, territory, term, affiliates, source/binary distribution, audit frequency, notice, confidentiality, record retention, royalties/fees, cure period, suspension/termination, indemnity allocation, dispute forum and reasonable verification costs after a material confirmed breach.

## Contractual penalty placeholder

No automatic amount is set here.

A negotiated agreement may contain a clause such as:

> If the Licensee culpably breaches a specifically identified material obligation, the contractual penalty shall be the amount expressly stated in the signed Order Form or Commercial License, subject to mandatory law and judicial adjustment. Any supplementary damages are recoverable only where expressly agreed and legally proven.

This placeholder intentionally avoids an arbitrary number. In Brazil, contractual penalties are subject to Civil Code limits and possible judicial reduction.

## Infringement outside contract

An alleged infringer who never assented to the commercial contract should not be described as automatically owing its contractual penalty. Available copyright/software remedies depend on applicable law, evidence and judicial process.
