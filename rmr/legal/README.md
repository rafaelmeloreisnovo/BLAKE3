# RMR legal/documentation boundary

State: DOCUMENTATION_ACTIVE / LICENSE_CHANGE_NOT_ACTIVATED

This directory separates legal facts, proposed licensing policy and technical provenance.

## Critical current-state fact

`rmr/LICENSE_RMR` currently grants broad MIT-style rights, including use, copy, modification, distribution, sublicensing and sale.

Therefore:

~~~text
CURRENT_GRANT != FUTURE_RESTRICTION
PAST_LICENSED_COPY != RETROACTIVELY_REVOKED_COPY
RMR_AUTHORED_MATERIAL != UPSTREAM_BLAKE3
~~~

No document in this directory silently revokes rights already granted under a prior license version. A future research-free/commercial-restricted regime must be activated prospectively, with a clear effective commit/date and legal review.

Upstream BLAKE3 remains under its upstream licenses and cannot be commercially restricted by RMR documentation.
